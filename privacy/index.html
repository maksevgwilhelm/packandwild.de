2. Hosting — GitHub Pages

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation).

When you visit this website, GitHub's servers automatically collect and store information in server log files. This includes:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of the request
  • Name and URL of the requested file
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL (previously visited page)

This data is processed by GitHub on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests) for the purpose of ensuring stable and secure server operation. We have no direct influence over this data collection by GitHub. For more information, see GitHub's privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data transfer to the USA is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision by the European Commission, July 2023), under which GitHub/Microsoft is certified.

3. Fonts — self-hosted, no external transfer

This website uses the typefaces Inter and Playfair Display. These fonts are stored directly on our server (GitHub Pages) and served from there. No connection to Google servers or any other external font service is made when you visit this website. No personal data is transferred to third parties as part of font loading.

4. External links & Pinterest

This website currently contains no active affiliate tracking links. Product pages contain placeholder links only — no click data is collected, no commissions are tracked, and no third-party affiliate network (such as AWIN) is integrated.

The website links to our Pinterest profile (pinterest.com/packandwild). When you follow this link and visit Pinterest, Pinterest, Inc. processes your data in accordance with their own privacy policy. Pinterest may set cookies and collect usage data. For details, see: Pinterest Privacy Policy.

Should active affiliate tracking links be introduced on this website in the future, this section will be updated prior to deployment to describe the processing activities, legal basis, and opt-out options.

5. localStorage — language preference

This website stores your language preference (English or German) in your browser's localStorage. This is a technical storage mechanism in your browser — it is not a cookie, does not transmit data to any server, and is not subject to cookie consent requirements under the ePrivacy Directive.

The stored key is paw-lang with a value of either "en" or "de". You can clear this at any time through your browser settings (Clear Site Data / Local Storage).

6. Analytics

This website does not currently use any web analytics service (no Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no heat-map tools). If analytics are introduced in the future, this privacy policy will be updated prior to deployment.

7. Your rights under GDPR

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR) — you may request information about the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) — you may request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) — you may request deletion of your data
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR) — you may object to processing based on legitimate interests

Note: PackAndWild does not directly collect or store personal data beyond what is technically logged by the hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages). Your rights relating to server log data should therefore be directed to GitHub.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority in Germany is the Bundesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit (BfDI): bfdi.bund.de.
